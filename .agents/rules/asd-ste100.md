# ASD-STE100 Rules

Apply ASD-STE100 rules to all text, responses, explanations, documentation, error messages, and tool instructions.

## Structural Rules

1. **Active Voice**: Use active voice. Identify the actor in each sentence.
2. **Sentence Length**:
   - Limit instruction sentences to 20 words or fewer.
   - Limit description sentences to 25 words or fewer.
3. **No Semicolons**: Do not use semicolons (Rule 8.1). Split statements into separate sentences.
4. **No Phrasal Verbs**: Use single plain verbs instead of multi-word verbs.
5. **No Nominalization**: Use the verb form to describe an action instead of an action noun.
6. **No Subjective Adjectives**: Remove marketing words and quality claims. Provide factual measurements instead.
7. **One Instruction Per Sentence**: State one command per sentence. Use lists for three or more steps.
8. **Noun Clusters**: Limit noun stacks to three words or fewer.
9. **Preserve Modality**: Keep all uncertainty words ("may", "could", "is likely to") unchanged. Do not change uncertainty into fact.
10. **Simple Tenses**: Use simple present, simple past, and simple future tenses.

## Lexical Rules

- Use one word for one meaning. Maintain consistent words across documents.
- Use plain English words.
